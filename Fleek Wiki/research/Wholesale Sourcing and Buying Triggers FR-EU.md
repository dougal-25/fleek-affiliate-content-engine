# Wholesale Sourcing and Buying Triggers FR-EU

> How FR/EU resellers buy wholesale today, what they hate about it, and what makes them switch supplier — Fleek's competitive context and the outreach ammunition.
Part of [[index]] · siblings: [[Fleek Company Profile]], [[FR Resale Platform Landscape]]

### How they source today

- **B2B grossistes** dominate: palettes (100kg–1t) and containers, machine + hand sorted. Named players: Amiratex, Actifrip, Eureka Fripe (36,000 m² warehouse, 24h shipping), Fripe'In Store United, DHEM (brand-sorted: Zara/Levi's/Maje, −40% from 50 pieces), BCR (par kilo) — [europages](https://www.europages.fr/entreprises/grossiste-en-friperie.html), [eureka-fripe](https://www.eureka-fripe.com), [dhem](https://dhem-france.com/pages/grossiste-friperie-marque)
- Typical EU delivery 7–14 days; MOQs from ~50 items for brand lots

### What they complain about (= Fleek's opening lines)

- **Inconsistent quality** despite "Grade A" claims → return rates >5%
- **Opaque sorting** — can't verify what's actually in a bale before it lands
- **Unreliable delivery** past the 7–14 day benchmark, esp. cross-border
- **High MOQs / rigid payment terms** straining small-reseller cash flow
- Limited availability of specific brands/grades → over-ordering to be safe
*(complaint set largely from one aggregated source — treat individual items as unverified until echoed in creator content)*

### What makes them choose / switch a supplier

- **Triggers:** verified quality via sample orders; per-piece selection (not closed packs); EU-warehouse speed; transparent pricing incl. shipping/customs; ethical certifications; verifiable physical warehouse; reviews at volume — [albanos](https://albanosonline.com/en/blog/best-wholesale-clothing-suppliers-in-europe-b99.html)
- **Rejections:** closed-pack mandates, no references, hidden fees, no local-language support
- **Switching pattern:** small trial order first (often the MOQ), then scale — switching happens when staying costs more than moving

### Outreach/brief ammunition derived

1. Lead outreach with the **sourcing pain**, not the commission: "verify before you buy" beats "earn 10%".
2. Brief angle: **bale-unboxing as quality-transparency content** — the exact complaint (opaque sorting) becomes the content format.
3. Trial-order psychology → the creator offer should have a low-commitment first step.
