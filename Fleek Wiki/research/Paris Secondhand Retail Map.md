### The 5-tier taxonomy of Paris secondhand
A genuinely useful framework for *where* secondhand happens and at what price — [Style Paris](https://styleparis.substack.com/p/the-only-paris-second-hand-shopping):

| Tier | What | Price signal | Examples named |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Friperies** | High-volume, hit-or-miss, dig-required | €1–€100 | Guerrisol, Hippy Market, Free'P'Star, Kilo Shop |
| **Boutiques solidaires / ressourceries** | Charity/thrift, good for basics + menswear | under €20 | Emmaüs, Bis Boutique Solidaire |
| **Dépôt-ventes (consignment)** | More selective, pricier, "chaotic" | mid–high | (category) |
| **Appointment-only** | Archival, for pros/stylists/collectors | €90–€700+ | Turn Paris |
| **Curated secondhand boutiques** | The sweet spot — edited, aesthetic-led | €50–€500 | NUOVO, Erem, Aimé, Iconic, Gaijin, Kanelle, Snow Bunny |

### The Marais / Rue de Turenne cluster
**7 of 9 curated shops sit on or beside Rue de Turenne** (3e) — NUOVO, Erem, Aimé, Iconic, Snow Bunny, Gaijin, Kanelle. This is the walkable epicentre of curated Paris resale, ringed by content-friendly spots (Marché des Enfants Rouges, Café Charlot, The Broken Arm, Place des Vosges). **For the engine: this is where FR resale content gets filmed** — the "geo-tagged Paris haul" format ([[FR Creator Content Formats]]) has a literal address.

### FR retail vocabulary surfaced
- **friperie** — thrift/secondhand shop (dig-it-yourself)
- **dépôt-vente** — consignment shop
- **boutique solidaire / ressourcerie** — charity/reuse shop (Emmaüs model)
- **kilo shop** — sold by weight
