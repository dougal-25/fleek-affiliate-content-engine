### What makes creators agree to promote (FR-specific)
- **France = coupon-first, not influencer-first**: coupon-based affiliate models are the most popular in FR (56%); influencer-led the least (36%) — creators prefer performance/discount-aligned deals over pure storytelling — [Awin](https://www.awin.com/gb/sector-insights/affiliate-marketing-global-views)
- **Relationship management is the #1 FR challenge** (vs brand-consistency globally) — creators reject programs with poor communication/support — [Awin](https://www.awin.com/gb/sector-insights/affiliate-marketing-global-views)
- Yes-triggers: **generous commission + long cookie + exclusive perks** (samples, early access) + a **trustworthy brand** — [creator-hero](https://www.creator-hero.com/blog/best-affiliate-programs-in-france)
- Instant no's: **pay-to-play** joining fees, vague tracking, "get rich quick" / MLM-style recruitment — [YT](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RbVEWfCKLMA)
- FR network **Affilae** offers Target Plans (brand hand-picks creators, more support) vs Open Plans (scale); creators prefer Target for alignment — [Affilae](https://affilae.com/en/affilae-tiktok-shop-partner-affiliate-marketing/)

### Tiering models (Bronze / Silver / Gold)
Performance thresholds unlock escalating commission. Real examples — [affonso](https://affonso.io/resources/affiliate-tier-commission-planner), [Logos](https://support.logos.com/hc/en-us/articles/34253009964557):

| Tier | Threshold (example) | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Bronze | entry / 0–10 sales/mo | 5–20% |
| Silver | 11–25 sales/mo (or 15+) | 10–30% |
| Gold | 26+ sales/mo (or 30+) | 20–40% |

- **Rank-based alternative** (Logos): Gold = top 10% earners, Platinum = top 2%, re-evaluated **every 90 days**.
- Design rules: thresholds attainable-but-motivating; jumps must feel meaningful (10→15→20%); re-assess quarterly.

### Payout models & friction
- **Escalating frequency by trust**: new affiliates on monthly **net-30** → proven performers (3–6 mo) get **weekly** → top VIPs (1yr+) get **on-demand/daily** — [zonflip](https://zonflip.com/the-payout-mechanics-that-actually-keep-top-creators-in-your-affiliate-program/)
- **Rolling-revenue tiers**: e.g. 15% after $5k/30d, 18% after $15k/mo, 20–25%+ custom above $30k/mo
- **2026 benchmarks by category**: physical goods (fashion/beauty) **5–15%**; digital 20–50%; subscriptions 15–30% recurring — [Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/blog/affiliate-commission)
- **Friction to design out**: net-30 cash-flow gaps for new affiliates; cumulative-vs-rolling threshold confusion; return/fraud hold periods (30–60d); no real-time dashboard for tier progress.

### Activation — signup → first sale (the highest-leverage stage)
The one metric that matters (per Fleek's own JD) is **activation rate**. Tactics that move the needle — [Reddit r/AffiliateMarket](https://www.reddit.com/r/AffiliateMarket/comments/1p5mshw/), [rewardful](https://www.rewardful.com/guides/affiliate-onboarding):
1. **First-Sale Fast-Track Challenge** — time-bound ("first sale in 14 days") + modest bonus (cash / rate bump / feature). The single most effective tactic.
2. **Product-seeding demo tasks** — assign a walkthrough within 7 days; provide templates; feature top submissions → reusable content early.
3. **Milestone onboarding** — share link → first click → first sale → 3 sales/30d, each rewarded (badges, early payout).
4. **Buddy system** — pair newcomers with a mentor for 30 days; mentor earns a bonus if the newcomer activates.
5. **Revenue-share sprint** — temporary commission double for first 30 days (urgency, no permanent margin loss).
6. **High-touch personalisation** — 1:1 call, co-created promo plan, swipe copy + deep-link tutorials.

### TikTok Shop FR commission reference
Seller-set, **1–80%**; fashion/vintage typically **10–20% open**, **15–25% targeted**, up to **50%** for proven volume creators (US avg 13.02%, FR uses same flexible model) — [bemomentiq](https://bemomentiq.com/blog/tiktok-shop-affiliate-commission-rates-complete-breakdown-by), [hamstergarage](https://www.hamstergarage.com/article/tiktok-shop-affiliate-commission-rates-fees-payouts). *(Detailed on [[TikTok Shop France]].)*
