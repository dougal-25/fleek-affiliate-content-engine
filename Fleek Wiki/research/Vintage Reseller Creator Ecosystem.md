## Where creators sit relative to Fleek

Fleek is one layer **up** from these creators — it's where they buy graded bundles/bales (MOQs from 10 pieces, BNPL, buyer protection) to then resell on Vinted/Depop/Whatnot and film about. — [Fleek how-it-works](https://www.joinfleek.com/pages/how-it-works)

## Creator tiers & economics

- **Tiers by followers**: nano 1K–10K (**67% of all creators**), micro 10K–50K, mid 50K–500K, macro 500K+. Per-post earnings ~$10–100 (nano) up to $10K+ (macro/mega). — [inBeat](https://inbeat.agency/blog/creator-economy-statistics)
- Creator economy context: ~207M active creators, market ~$191B (2025) → $528B (2030) *(unverified, single source)*. — [Influencer Marketing Factory](https://theinfluencermarketingfactory.com/creator-economy/)
- **TikTok Shop affiliate commissions ~5–30%** of order value by category *(unverified)*. — [Influencer Advisory](https://influenceradvisory.com/blog/tiktok-shop-creator-affiliate/)
- **FR unit economics** (concrete): grossiste lot ~€8/piece → resold on Vinted ~€25 → ~€17 margin/item; a 50-piece lot nets ~€850 gross. "Vinted's algorithm rewards restocking regularity." — [fripeinstore](https://fripeinstoreunited.fr/en/blogs/infos/ou-les-vendeurs-vinted-trouvent-leurs-vetements-guide-complet-pour-les-revendeurs)

## Content formats that perform

1. **Wholesale/bale unboxing** — creator opens a sealed mystery bale on camera, reacts item-by-item, does cost-per-piece math at the end. Durable sub-genre (#wholesalehaul, #clothing-bales; standing accounts like Mr Kevin's RagHouse), not a fad. — [#wholesalehaul](https://www.tiktok.com/tag/wholesalehaul)
2. **Rag-house-as-destination** — operators (e.g. The Raghouse, Las Vegas) run live public bale-digging events; the *sourcing trip* is the content *(unverified, single snippet)*.
3. **Thrift haul / "come thrifting with me"** — sequential item-reveal-and-price + first-person store walkthroughs; backbone of #thrifting. No niche-specific view benchmarks pulled this sweep — general UGC stats (92% trust UGC over branded ads; ~70% of Gen Z discover via influencers) explain *why* it works. — [StackInfluence](https://stackinfluence.com/creator-economy-tiktok-e-commerce-trends-2025/) *(unverified)*
4. **Grossiste-run accounts** — FR wholesalers (Vinqa, bartorico_) post bale-opening clips as their *own* top-of-funnel marketing, blurring wholesaler and creator. — [Vinqa](https://www.tiktok.com/@vinqa.grossiste/video/7452030748871167254)
5. **Live selling** — Whatnot / TikTok / IG Live is France's volume channel, distinct from Vinted (daily drip) and Leboncoin (local lots) *(synthesised, no viewership data)*.

## Why a creator promotes a sourcing platform (motivation model)

Three stacking reasons — the third is the strategic one:
1. **The format sells itself.** Bale unboxing *is* good content — the platform supplies the "mystery box"; no separate ad needed.
2. **Operational leverage.** Sourcing is explicitly the most time-consuming part of a reseller's business (per Fleek's own copy) — a creator who also runs a shop wins time (less thrifting legwork, more filming/listing) *independent of any commission*.
3. **Commission layers cleanly on top.** TikTok-Shop-style 5–30% cuts, or free bundle credit for a haul video, bolt onto a format that already exists organically.

> This is the seed of the Fleek wedge: creators here are *already buyers*. See [[Competitor Creator Programs]] "structural gap" and [[index]].

## Regional note — France compliance friction

Casual vide-dressing (selling your own closet) is informal/untaxed, but **buying stock to resell for margin, done regularly, legally needs business registration (SIRET)** — a framing question for how FR affiliate creators present themselves. — [MicroChrono](https://microchrono.fr/blog/devenir-revendeur-seconde-main-auto-entrepreneur/) *(unverified)*. Vinted has recruited influencers to list their own wardrobes as an acquisition tactic. — [Gens d'Internet](https://gensdinternet.fr/2023/04/20/retrouvez-les-vide-dressing-sur-vinted-des-influenceurs/) *(unverified)*
