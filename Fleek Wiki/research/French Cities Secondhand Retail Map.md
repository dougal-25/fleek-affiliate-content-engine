# French Cities Secondhand Retail Map

### The national kilo-pricing standard
The **Kilo Shop** colour-tier scale recurs across every major city and is the de-facto standard: **Red €20/kg · Green €30/kg · Blue €40/kg · Orange €60/kg**. Budget kilo alternatives run cheaper — KiloChic Bordeaux €5–15/kg, Fringokilo Lille ~€20/kg. Per-piece budget floors: €2 bins (Marseille, Strasbourg €3). Curated per-piece typically €10–50. → useful anchor for pricing/margin reasoning and brief realism.

### City hubs (where secondhand concentrates, and why)
- **Lyon** → Croix-Rousse / Pentes (1er), historic silk district, highest friperie density; Puces du Canal (Villeurbanne) is the flea anchor.
- **Marseille** → Cours Julien / La Plaine (13006): rue Pastoret + rue des Trois Mages + rue de Lodi. 50+ shops; strong OM-jersey/sportswear sub-niche.
- **Bordeaux** → Rue du Loup (curated) + Rue Sainte-Catherine (budget/charity) + Saint-Michel/Rue du Mirail (digging).
- **Lille** → Vieux-Lille (premium), République-Beaux-Arts + Rue Gambetta (mid), Wazemmes (budget). Supercharged annually by the Braderie.
- **Nantes** → Decré/Pilori/Rue de la Marne core; unusually strong solidaire/social-economy presence (Le Relais, suspended-clothing schemes).
- **Toulouse** → Place de la Bourse/Rue Cujas/Rue Peyrolières + Saint-Cyprien; notable US-import streetwear supply.
- **Strasbourg** → Krutenau (student quarter) is the vintage engine; cross-border Germanic 90s-branded sportswear niche.

### The events that matter
- **Marché de la Mode Vintage (MMV), Lyon** — La Sucrière, Confluence. **Twice yearly (March + early autumn)**, ~180 exhibitors, 3,800 m². Founded March 2001 — France's pioneer vintage salon; 30th-anniversary edition 7–8 March 2026. Runs a "Lyon Vintage Tour" of friperies.
- **Braderie de Lille** — **First weekend of September** (2026: 5–6 Sep). Europe's largest flea market: ~100 km of stalls, ~10,000 exhibitors, ~2M visitors.
- **Toulouse Vintage** (Hôtel-Dieu Saint-Jacques); **Puces du Canal** (Lyon, permanent); **Place des Tripiers** flea (Strasbourg, Wed & Sat).
