# Competitor Creator Programs

> How rival resale platforms run (or fail to run) creator/affiliate programs — and the structural gap Fleek can exploit. This is the differentiation map for the case study.

Part of [[index]] · siblings: [[Fleek Company Profile]] · [[Vintage Reseller Creator Ecosystem]] · [[Affiliate Program Playbooks]]

## The headline: the wedge no competitor can copy

> **No competitor pays a creator to both *earn commission* and *build inventory at wholesale* in the same relationship.** Every program below rewards *either* driving traffic/sales *or* making content — never both, and none let a creator buy stock at a wholesale rate as part of the deal. As a wholesale-sourcing marketplace, **Fleek can fuse affiliate commission with actual sourcing margin** — something none of these six can copy without also becoming a wholesale supplier.

Two more open flanks:
- **Vinted** (Europe's biggest resale platform) has **no native creator/affiliate program at all.**
- **eBay's** unit economics are weak enough (1–4%, 24-hr cookie, hard caps) that "we pay better and longer" is trivially substantiable.

## Detail worth keeping

- **Whatnot** is the benchmark to beat: personalised link + 10% ceiling / 65-day validation; a no-follower-floor UGC bounty ($300 shopping credit or $200 cash via Lumanu); and seller sales bonuses (Rising $250→$50, Power $750→$150, Super $2,500→$500). — [vCommission](https://www.vcommission.com/blog/publishers/whatnot-affiliate-program-capitalize-on-high-intent-buyers-through-live-auctions/), [Whatnot creator program](https://whatnotcreatorprogram.com/)
- **Depop Ambassador**: 18+, event-planning/social experience preferred, US-only, selectively recruited, pay undisclosed. — [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/depop-launches-ambassador-program/)
- Cross-platform recruiting signal: a reseller on the **Mercari** subreddit mentioned being invited to interview for Depop's Ambassador Program — platforms recruit from the **same shared pool** of cross-platform resellers, who **compare programs openly**. — Value Added Resource

## Transparency gap Fleek can own

Depop won't disclose ambassador pay, Whatnot's UGC selection odds are unstated, ThredUp's rate varies by network with no canonical number. **A program that publishes clear, fixed payout terms stands out purely by contrast.**

## 🚨 2026-07-09 post-level evidence — Whatnot's FR creator programme is ACTIVE and dominant

Not a hypothetical benchmark. Measured in the top creators' own posts ([[Creator Post-Level Signals]]):
- **72 `whatnot.com` links** in their YouTube descriptions — **Julia Courcelle (50)**, **Alex Yedder (22)**. Whatnot is *paying/partnering* the exact creators Fleek wants.
- On TikTok, **`#whatnotpartner` posts carry a median 786,250 plays** (n=4) — **the highest-performing hashtag in the entire 300-post sample** (next best: `#storytime`, 219k). Partner content doesn't just exist, it outperforms everything.
- `whatnot` appears **123×** across YouTube titles/descriptions and **29×** on TikTok.

→ **Whatnot is the live competitor for creator attention, not a paper comparison.** Any Fleek programme is competing for the same slots in the same descriptions. Note the flip side: Whatnot pays for *content/links*; it still cannot offer **wholesale sourcing margin** — the fusion wedge holds, but the attention fight is real and already underway.

## 2026-07-09 enrichment — Whatnot FR/EU economics + resale-affiliate landscape

- **Whatnot operates in France/EU** at **6.67% + VAT** seller commission (vs 8% US/CA/AU); electronics 5%; **0% on the portion over €1,500** (limited-time); **tips 0% commission** (seller keeps 100%). Affiliate side: link-sharing commission with a **72-hour (3-day) cookie**. — [crosslist](https://crosslist.com/blog/whatnot-fees-for-sellers), [whatnotaffiliates](https://www.whatnotaffiliates.com)
- **Vinted still has no official affiliate program** — only user-to-user referral vouchers ($5–15) and third-party SaaS tools (Redrip.app: 30% mo1–3 then 20% lifetime on seller *subscription* renewals, 60-day cookie). Vinted itself charges sellers **no sales commission** (revenue = buyer-protection + shipping fees). — [redrip](https://www.redrip.app/en/affiliate), [sharetribe](https://www.sharetribe.com/how-to-build/how-does-vinted-make-money/)
- **Vestiaire Collective, Depop, ThredUp**: no affiliate commission data surfaced this sweep (still gaps). *(unverified — absence of data, not confirmed absence of program.)*
- **Takeaway**: Whatnot is the FR live-selling benchmark but pays creators only on *content/links*, never on *sourcing* — the fusion wedge holds even in-market. → full mechanics on [[Creator Affiliate Mechanics]] and [[Supplier and B2B Referral Mechanics]].
