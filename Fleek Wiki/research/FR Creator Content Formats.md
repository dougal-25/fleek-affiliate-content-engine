# FR Creator Content Formats

> What thrift/reseller content is working on TikTok/IG right now — rising vs fading. Directly shapes brief generation.
Part of [[index]] · siblings: [[FR Reseller Vocabulary and Hashtags]], [[Vintage Reseller Creator Ecosystem]]

### Key facts

- **Thrift hauls are the dominant format** — #thrifthaul/#thriftflip families at 5.1B+ combined TikTok views; top creators convert up to 18% of viewers to resale clicks within 48h — [amraandelma](https://www.amraandelma.com/influencers-with-viral-thrift-store-hauls/) *(single source for conversion figure, unverified)*
- **Rising:** hauls **with storytelling** (unique positioning, struggle narratives) over repetitive generic hauls; **sourcing-trip vlogs** from named cities (Paris, Nice) showing designer access
- **Fading:** generic repetitive hauls with no angle; pure live selling *without* supporting haul/vlog content
- "Cozy lifestyle" framing (haul as coffee-chat) used to build authenticity
- Featured items reportedly sell **3.2× faster** post-viral-haul on resale platforms *(single source, unverified)*
- Search interest "thrifted outfit inspo" +37% YoY *(unverified)*

### Brief-generation rules derived

1. Never brief a generic haul — always attach an angle (sourcing story, transformation, economics reveal).
2. Sourcing-trip content is the natural Fleek showcase: **the bale unboxing IS the product demo.**
3. Live selling briefs only for creators with existing live capability ([[FR Resale Platform Landscape]] — Whatnot signal).
4. Storytelling beats polish — brief hooks around the creator's own journey.

### 2026-07-09 Reddit/Quora enrichment — what actually drives haul engagement + audience demand

**Engagement drivers on haul/find content** (r/ThriftStoreHauls via aggregator; raw in [[reddit_quora_threads_2026-07-09]]) — consistent, and directly briefable:
1. **The price IS the hook.** Titles are literally the number: *"$55 for all 4. I'm done. I've peaked."* / *"You guyssssss. $30."*
2. **Designer-name × tiny-price contrast** — the top reaction bait: *"People out here just casually donating a $328 sweater to goodwill"*; *"$500 bag... for $8."*
3. **The "looked it up when I got home" resale-value reveal** — buyer discovers a find is worth 10–20×. This is the exact bridge from *shopper* to *reseller* mindset — the psychological on-ramp Fleek sells into.
4. **"White whale" long-hunt payoffs** — years-long searches ending in triumph; treasure-hunt framing.
5. **Emotional / life-context finds** (hard times, omens, reunited sets) punch above their weight — community responds to the story.
6. **"Built to last" vintage-nostalgia** — durability/patina praised over newness.

**What buyers value** (ranked): the steal (price) > authenticity (real vs dupe; the discontinued-luxury-subbrand hack — Fendissime, Kenzo Jungle — chosen for lower fake risk) > uniqueness > heritage quality > ethics (stronger in France).

**Audience question-demand (Quora, snippet-level — brief-topic fuel):** French demand skews to **sourcing** ("comment se fournissent les friperies / où acheter en gros / au kilo") and **legal-tax** ("achat-revente légal, plafond, auto-entrepreneur"); English demand to **flipping economics** ("how much can you make", "worth full-time") and **item-selection** ("check eBay *sold* listings, specialise in one category"). *(unverified — Quora answer bodies were login-walled; questions are the confident signal.)* → these are ready-made brief topics.
