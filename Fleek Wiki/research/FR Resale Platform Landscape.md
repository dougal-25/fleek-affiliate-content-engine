# FR Resale Platform Landscape

> Where French fashion resellers actually sell in 2026 — platforms, fees, and who lives where. Tells the engine which platform signals mark a *professional* reseller.
Part of [[index]] · siblings: [[TikTok Shop France]], [[FR Reseller Vocabulary and Hashtags]], [[Wholesale Sourcing and Buying Triggers FR-EU]]

### Key facts

- **Vinted** dominates volume/static reselling: **0% seller fees** (buyer pays ~€0.70 + 5% protection), full-time sellers reportedly €2,000–€10,000/month — [vintieplus](https://vintieplus.com/blog/how-vinteds-fee-system-works-in-europe-why-sellers-pay-nothing-while-buyers-cover-everything/), [underpriced](https://www.underpriced.app/blog/vinted-selling-guide-europe-2026)
- Volume Vinted resellers deliberately **mimic "closet cleanouts"** to avoid commercial-seller flags; cross-listing tools (e.g. Closo) sync Vinted/eBay/Poshmark — [closo](https://closo.co/blogs/blog/vinted-reselling-the-zero-fee-loophole-that-changed-my-business) *(single source, unverified)*
- **Vestiaire Collective** = luxury/authenticated lane: 10–15% commission + authentication fees; runs curated **live luxury drops** *(unverified fee detail)*
- **Whatnot France** = the live-selling hub: ~8% + ~3% processing; streetwear/Y2K/vintage communities; France launch 2024 *(unverified)*
- **Leboncoin** = local/general, low structure; **eBay FR** = fee-heavy (10–13%) backup for collectors/cross-border
- **Live selling adoption:** high on Whatnot + Vestiaire, absent on Vinted/Leboncoin/eBay FR

### What this means for creator scoring

- A creator selling on **Vinted at volume + posting sourcing content** = professional reseller in disguise (the "closet cleanout" camouflage means bio/platform links understate scale).
- **Whatnot FR presence** = strong live-selling capability signal → different brief format (live sourcing hauls).
- **Vestiaire presence** = luxury lane, likely poor fit for bulk wholesale (different economics).
