# FR Creator Content Formats

> What thrift/reseller content is working on TikTok/IG right now — rising vs fading. Directly shapes brief generation.
Part of [[FLEEK BRAIN Index]] · siblings: [[FR Reseller Vocabulary and Hashtags]], [[Vintage Reseller Creator Ecosystem]]

### Key facts

- **Thrift hauls are the dominant format** — #thrifthaul/#thriftflip families at 5.1B+ combined TikTok views; top creators convert up to 18% of viewers to resale clicks within 48h — [amraandelma](https://www.amraandelma.com/influencers-with-viral-thrift-store-hauls/) *(single source for conversion figure, unverified)*
- **Rising:** hauls **with storytelling** (unique positioning, struggle narratives) over repetitive generic hauls; **sourcing-trip vlogs** from named cities (Paris, Nice) showing designer access
- **Fading:** generic repetitive hauls with no angle; pure live selling *without* supporting haul/vlog content
- "Cozy lifestyle" framing (haul as coffee-chat) used to build authenticity
- Featured items reportedly sell **3.2× faster** post-viral-haul on resale platforms *(single source, unverified)*
- Search interest "thrifted outfit inspo" +37% YoY *(unverified)*

### Brief-generation rules derived

1. Never brief a generic haul — always attach an angle (sourcing story, transformation, economics reveal).
2. Sourcing-trip content is the natural Fleek showcase: **the bale unboxing IS the product demo.**
3. Live selling briefs only for creators with existing live capability ([[FR Resale Platform Landscape]] — Whatnot signal).
4. Storytelling beats polish — brief hooks around the creator's own journey.
