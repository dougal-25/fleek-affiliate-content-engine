## What Fleek is

B2B wholesale marketplace connecting secondhand/vintage clothing **suppliers** (wholesalers, rag-houses, donation centres) with **resellers/retailers** who sell on to consumers. Fleek sits one layer *up* from the creators — it's where resellers buy their stock, not a consumer resale app. — [Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/fleek)

## How the marketplace works

- **Supply side**: suppliers list inventory (by weight, brand, style, size, material) as bundles, bales, or single "handpicks"; graded/QC'd at hubs before shipment. MOQs as low as 10 pieces. — [How it works](https://www.joinfleek.com/pages/how-it-works)
- **Buy side**: resellers browse curated listings, use "**Make an Offer**" to negotiate on items or whole bundles, and get AI-matched supplier recs via an in-app assistant, "**Fleeky**". — [How it works](https://www.joinfleek.com/pages/how-it-works)
- **Trust/finance**: Buyer Protection Policy; **BNPL** deferring payment up to 30 days post-receipt; ~10–14 working-day shipping with Fleek handling customs. — [How it works](https://www.joinfleek.com/pages/how-it-works)
- **Revenue**: commission on the buyer's payment to the supplier, varying by volume/quality (exact rate undisclosed, unverified). — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/11/12/fleek-a-marketplace-for-wholesale-second-hand-clothes-sews-up-20m/)

## Creator / affiliate motion today (thin)

- **⚠️ Disambiguation trap**: a "Fleek Affiliate Program" at **getfleek.app** (up to 50% subscription commission) is an **unrelated Web3/hosting company**. Do NOT conflate with joinfleek.com.
- What joinfleek.com actually runs is a **referral/discount-code system**, not a formal affiliate program: codes in the format `RFD-[username]`, ~£25–$35 off a referred buyer's first order, shared by creators on TikTok/IG/FB, plus periodic giveaways (leaderboard, £300 gift-card prize for top referrers). — [TikTok @joinfleek](https://www.tiktok.com/@joinfleek/video/7417883442064461088)
- The reseller-onboarding page leads with peer profit testimonials, **not** an affiliate structure — signalling Fleek's creator motion, if any, is early/thin. This is the gap the content engine is pitched into. — [new-reseller](https://www.joinfleek.com/en/resources/new-reseller)
