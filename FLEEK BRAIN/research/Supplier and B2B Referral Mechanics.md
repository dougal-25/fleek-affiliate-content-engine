# Supplier and B2B Referral Mechanics

> How wholesale/B2B suppliers and marketplaces run referral programs — Fleek's closest analogues and the mechanics to borrow. Closes the founding-sweep "wholesale-supplier referral programs unswept" gap. Sourced 2026-07-09.
Part of [[FLEEK BRAIN Index]] · siblings: [[Affiliate Program Playbooks]], [[Creator Affiliate Mechanics]], [[Competitor Creator Programs]]

### Key facts — the strategic finding

**Bale/wholesale suppliers almost never run public referral or affiliate programs.** Traditional grossistes optimise on margin + volume, not commission incentives. Where programs exist, they're on *branded* adaptive/sustainable clothing (5–15% commission, 14–30 day cookies), not mixed-stock bale suppliers — [backlinko](https://backlinko.com/fashion-affiliate-programs), [adaptivecomfortclothing](https://www.adaptivecomfortclothing.com/pages/affiliate-and-wholesale). **This is the whitespace**: a wholesale marketplace running a real creator/referral program is close to unprecedented.

### B2B marketplace referral structures (Faire, Ankorstore, Alibaba)
The pattern across all three — **incentivise supply-side growth, not buyer-to-buyer**:

- **No buyer-to-buyer program.** Faire explicitly gives retailers *no* credit for referring other retailers — [Faire](https://www.faire.com/support/articles/36788664920987)
- **Brand-to-buyer discount**: new retailer signing via a brand's link gets a discount + **1 year free shipping** with that brand — [Faire retailer guide](https://www.faire.com/blog/for-retailers/retailer-guide-to-buying-on-faire)
- **Retailer-refers-brand credit**: up to **$2,000** ($50 on signup + $50/accepted order, first 30 days, ≤40 orders) — [Faire brand referrals](https://www.faire.com/user/brand-referrals)
- Ankorstore mirrors Faire; Alibaba is more fragmented (supplier→buyer discounts, no retailer credit program).

### Fleek's own referral mechanics today (the baseline to improve)
- Informal **`RFD-` / `FD-` codes**; **£25 off a £200 minimum** first order; earn credits per successful referral; early access to premium categories — [Fleek how-it-works](https://www.joinfleek.com/pages/how-it-works), [Reddit r/reselling](https://www.reddit.com/r/reselling/comments/1t4a5nu/wholesale_vintageused_clothing_on_a_budget_fleek/)
- ⚠️ **Trust complaint**: a promoted referral bonus later enforced an undisclosed $200 threshold and took 5 days vs the claimed 1 — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fleek-wholesale/id1631016145). *Lesson: transparency of terms is the first thing to fix.*

### B2B referral best practice (what to build)
- **Dual-payout (multi-step)**: reward for qualified lead (10–30% of CPL) + larger reward on conversion (3–20% of ACV); referred buyer gets an exclusive discount — [referralrock](https://referralrock.com/blog/b2b-referral-programs/)
- **Tiered**: escalating rewards by volume/quality → VIP perks (dedicated support) for top referrers
- **Non-monetary business benefits** often beat cash — B2B referrers want discounts, training, feature access, not "personal income" — [regie.ai](https://www.regie.ai/blog/5-best-practices-for-a-successful-b2b-referral-program)
- **Incentivise both parties**; use "Refer a business" CTAs, not generic "Share now".

### The commission-stacking warning
No documented case of a wholesale marketplace acquiring *buyers* via creator/affiliate channels — because platforms charging 25% first-order commission can't easily stack a creator fee on the same sale, and they own the buyer data — [luckybreakconsulting](https://www.luckybreakconsulting.com/review-of-faire-the-wholesale-marketplace-platform/). **Fleek's edge**: it doesn't need to stack — the creator *is* the buyer, so sourcing margin (not a separate commission budget) funds the incentive. This is the wedge, quantified.

### Open questions / gaps
- Direct-competitor (BankandVogue, other secondhand-wholesale) referral terms — search only returned Fleek's own.
