# TikTok Shop France

> TikTok Shop's FR state of play — the affiliate mechanics Fleek's creator program would ride on. Closes a founding-sweep backlog gap.
Part of [[FLEEK BRAIN Index]] · siblings: [[FR Resale Platform Landscape]], [[Affiliate Program Playbooks]]

### Key facts

- **Live in France since 31 March 2025**; physical goods incl. fashion, in-app checkout, Seller Center access — [economie-numerique](https://blog.economie-numerique.net/2025/04/12/tiktok-shop-in-france-a-revolution-in-social-commerce/)
- **81.7% affiliate dependency** in the FR market — creators are the primary distribution channel for fashion brands; self-operated sales lowest in Europe at 8.9% — [Lengow](https://blog.lengow.com/top-50-shops-on-tiktok-shop-in-europe/)
- **Highest average unit price in continental Europe: €31.98** — better per-item commission economics than neighbouring markets — [Lengow](https://blog.lengow.com/top-50-shops-on-tiktok-shop-in-europe/)
- Top FR fashion/wellness shop (Umay FR): **€772k GMV at 99% affiliate rate** — [Lengow](https://blog.lengow.com/top-50-shops-on-tiktok-shop-in-europe/)
- **"Sell Across Europe"**: FR products promotable cross-EU (DE/IT/ES), 200M+ users — [TikTok newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-shop-expands-across-europe?lang=en-150)
- Commission rates are brand-set; digital products unsupported

### Why this matters for the case study

The FR market is **structurally affiliate-first** — 4 in 5 GMV euros flow through creators, not brand shops. A creator-led acquisition engine isn't a nice-to-have in France; it's how the channel already works. This stat belongs ON the exec-summary slide.

### 2026-07-09 enrichment — commission %s + resale restrictions (both prior gaps, now closed)

**Creator-side commission % (fashion/vintage)** — rates are **seller-set, 1–80%**; fashion/vintage typically:
- **Open Plan** (creator self-selects): **10–20%** (often 10–15% standard apparel)
- **Targeted Plan** (seller invites): **15–25%**, up to **50%** for proven volume creators
- US all-category avg 13.02%; FR uses the same flexible model — no FR-specific fixed rates published — [bemomentiq](https://bemomentiq.com/blog/tiktok-shop-affiliate-commission-rates-complete-breakdown-by), [hamstergarage](https://www.hamstergarage.com/article/tiktok-shop-affiliate-commission-rates-fees-payouts)

**Resale/secondhand category restrictions (FR/EU) — this is a hard gate:**
- **Private sellers CANNOT list secondhand.** All resale requires **By-Application** onboarding, restricted to qualified *business* sellers (business docs, warehouse/stock photos, existing store links, certifications) — [TikTok Seller Univ.](https://seller-ie.tiktok.com/university/essay?default_language=en-GB&knowledge_id=748206502201108)
- **Second-hand fashion capped at €600/listing**; titles must say "second-hand/pre-owned/pre-loved"; ≥2 images incl. one showing imperfections
- Banned: secondhand underwear/socks/swimwear; EU-recalled items. Refurbished phones need a "Passed" certification report
- **VAT/EPR/GPSR**: EU VAT registration required; EPR auto-enrol at 1%/order without an EPR number; 14-day EU returns; GPSR compliance

**Implication for Fleek**: Fleek's creators are *businesses* (resellers), so they can clear the By-Application gate — a moat casual sellers can't cross. The €600 cap is a non-issue for bulk secondhand. This *reinforces* the pro-reseller wedge. → detail on [[Creator Affiliate Mechanics]].
