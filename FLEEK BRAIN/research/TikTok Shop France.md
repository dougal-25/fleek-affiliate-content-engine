# TikTok Shop France

> TikTok Shop's FR state of play — the affiliate mechanics Fleek's creator program would ride on. Closes a founding-sweep backlog gap.
Part of [[FLEEK BRAIN Index]] · siblings: [[FR Resale Platform Landscape]], [[Affiliate Program Playbooks]]

### Key facts

- **Live in France since 31 March 2025**; physical goods incl. fashion, in-app checkout, Seller Center access — [economie-numerique](https://blog.economie-numerique.net/2025/04/12/tiktok-shop-in-france-a-revolution-in-social-commerce/)
- **81.7% affiliate dependency** in the FR market — creators are the primary distribution channel for fashion brands; self-operated sales lowest in Europe at 8.9% — [Lengow](https://blog.lengow.com/top-50-shops-on-tiktok-shop-in-europe/)
- **Highest average unit price in continental Europe: €31.98** — better per-item commission economics than neighbouring markets — [Lengow](https://blog.lengow.com/top-50-shops-on-tiktok-shop-in-europe/)
- Top FR fashion/wellness shop (Umay FR): **€772k GMV at 99% affiliate rate** — [Lengow](https://blog.lengow.com/top-50-shops-on-tiktok-shop-in-europe/)
- **"Sell Across Europe"**: FR products promotable cross-EU (DE/IT/ES), 200M+ users — [TikTok newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-shop-expands-across-europe?lang=en-150)
- Commission rates are brand-set; digital products unsupported

### Why this matters for the case study

The FR market is **structurally affiliate-first** — 4 in 5 GMV euros flow through creators, not brand shops. A creator-led acquisition engine isn't a nice-to-have in France; it's how the channel already works. This stat belongs ON the exec-summary slide.
