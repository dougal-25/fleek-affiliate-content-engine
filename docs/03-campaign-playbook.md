# Campaign Playbook — Operating Cadence

## Segmentation

Every creator sits in exactly one cell of:

- **Tier:** nano (<10k) · micro (10–100k) · mid (100–500k) · macro (500k–1M) · mega (1M+)
- **Channel:** TikTok · YouTube · Instagram
- **Geo:** UK · FR
- **Niche:** vintage-fashion · thrift-hauls · reseller-education · streetwear · sustainability · live-selling
- **Lifecycle:** new · active · at-risk · dormant · core

Strategy, insights, and budget live at segment level; briefs live at creator level.

## The weekly rhythm (what Monday–Friday looks like)

| Day | Motion |
|---|---|
| Mon | Pull weekend performance; feedback agent produces segment insights; lifecycle transitions applied |
| Tue | Budget engine reallocates; kill-or-scale calls on live campaigns; WBR slide: activation %, CAC, payback, AOV |
| Wed | Activation targeting: next batch (due actives, at-risk saves, dormant batch, new-recruit onboarding); briefs generated |
| Thu | Briefs QA'd (spot-check 10%), sent; core-partner calls; hero-campaign work |
| Fri | Recruitment review (YouTube/IG vs reseller ICP); community bridge — winners into Discord, references into library |

## Campaign types

1. **Always-on activation** — monthly personalised brief to every active partner. Goal: keep
   the posting % up. Measured on posts shipped and buyers per post.
2. **Dormant re-activation** — batched by dormancy age. Different message: lead with what
   changed at Fleek + their own best past post + a warm incentive. Success = posted within
   14 days. Expect low rates (5–15%) — cheap wins because acquisition cost is sunk.
3. **Hero campaigns (2–3 per half)** — creator activations around a moment (e.g. FR community
   launch, seasonal resale peak). Core partners front it, long tail amplifies with
   pre-packaged brief variants.
4. **Onboarding sprints** — new recruits get a first-post brief within 48h of signing.
   Day-30 first-post rate is the KPI.

## Budget rules (the trading desk)

- **70/20/10:** 70% to proven segments (best CAC with volume confidence), 20% test budget for
  under-observed segments, 10% wild bets (new niches, formats, FR expansion pushes).
- **Per-campaign caps:** every campaign ships with a hypothesis, a cap, and a pre-agreed
  kill-or-scale line (e.g. "kill if CAC > 2× channel average after £500 spent").
- **Weekly reallocation:** rank segments by confidence-adjusted CAC; move budget stepwise
  (max ±25%/week per segment) to avoid whipsawing on noise.
- **Tiered gifting:** nano/micro → product gifting + affiliate %; mid → gifting + flat + %;
  macro/mega/core → negotiated contracts, owned by the manager not the engine.

## Kill-or-scale decision template

> **Hypothesis:** UK micro thrift-haul creators on TikTok will convert hobbyist resellers at
> CAC < £40 using "sourcing haul" format.
> **Budget cap:** £1,500. **Window:** 3 weeks.
> **Scale if:** CAC ≤ £40 with ≥ 20 first orders. **Kill if:** CAC > £80 at cap. **Iterate
> if:** in between — feedback agent proposes the variable to change (hook, offer, or CTA).

## The community bridge

- Every cycle's top 3 posts → Discord spotlight (UK + FR) + added to the reference library
  the brief generator draws from.
- Reactivated creators get a community touch (invite, ambassador track) within a week —
  re-activation sticks when there's belonging, not just incentive.
- Referral loop: high-NPS buyers surfaced to recruitment as creator leads (customer:influencer
  ratio trending up, per the JD).
